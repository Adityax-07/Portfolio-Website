AgentBench

Multi-agent LLM benchmarking · 2026
◖●◗ Source↗ Demo

problem A multi-agent system can sound smarter while quietly costing far more time. AgentBench makes the quality and latency tradeoff visible.

24% → 4% hallucination · 5.5s → 307s latency

Explore method & results
  • Designed a five-node pipeline with Planner, Researcher, Analyst, Writer and Memory, including Tavily tool calls, structured outputs and SQLite agent memory.
  • Benchmarked 50 queries across five metrics and two configurations; a resumable LLM-as-judge runner scored 100 responses.
  • Multi-agent execution cut hallucination sixfold, from 24% to 4%, at a 56× latency cost: 307 seconds versus 5.5 seconds.

RAG Systems Eval Suite

Eight-strategy RAG benchmark · 2026
◖●◗ Source↗ Demo

problem “Use RAG” is not a retrieval strategy. This suite compares eight approaches across quality, faithfulness and hallucination.

0.760 → 0.881 faithfulness · 4,000 answer-metric pairs

Explore method & results
  • Benchmarked eight strategies over 4,000 answer-metric pairs: 50 questions, eight systems and ten metrics.
  • FAISS + BM25/RRF hybrid search with cross-encoder reranking raised faithfulness from 0.760 to 0.881 and cut hallucination from 0.816 to 0.221.
  • Built a dual-provider LLM-as-judge with SQLite checkpoint and resume; published the work as an arXiv paper in cs.IR.

RAG Security Defense

LLM guardrails & hybrid retrieval · 2026
◖●◗ Source↗ Demo

problem Retrieval systems need defenses against prompt injection, jailbreaks and access-control leaks.

30/30 labeled attacks blocked · zero cross-tier leaks

Explore method & results
  • Built a five-layer defense pipeline that blocked all 30 labeled attacks in the test set, with under 1 ms first-layer latency.
  • Combined dense retrieval, BM25, RRF and cross-encoder reranking with three-tier RBAC; observed zero cross-tier leaks in a 100-query suite.

CodeSage

LLM vs RAG vs fine-tuning · 2026
◖●◗ Source↗ Demo

problem The right LLM setup depends on accuracy, hallucination, cost and latency. CodeSage puts the options side by side.

85.3% accuracy · $0.0002 per query

Explore method & results
